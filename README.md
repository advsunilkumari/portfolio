## ⚖️ Features & Compliance
- **Bar Council of India Rule 36 Compliance**: Prominent ethics disclaimer modal informing visitors that the site is strictly informational and for scheduling, with zero solicitation, advertising, or guarantees of legal outcomes.
- **Evening Chamber Consultations (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM)**: Interactive slot picker allowing clients to schedule In-Person Chamber visits or Virtual Video Consultations after daytime court hearings.
- **Calendar (.ics) Slip Generator**: Generates an `.ics` appointment file ready to add directly to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.
- **Privacy & Security**: **No phone number is displayed**, protecting confidentiality and routing inquiries through verified appointments and official email (`sukumari3128@gmail.com`).
- **Interactive Map Placeholder**: Embedded location placeholder for the Rajasthan High Court Bench & Sessions Court Complex, Jaipur.
